## Problem: Two Sum (Easy)

**Link:** https://leetcode.com/problems/two-sum/

### Approach

I used a nested loop to check every possible pair of numbers. If the sum of two numbers equals the target, their indices are returned.

### Complexity

- Time: O(n²)
- Space: O(1)

### Notes

The solution checks all possible pairs and handles cases where the required pair is not found.
